## Question

The two trained mechanism probes quoted in `paper/sections/experiments.tex` §4.3 (delayed-copy
per-channel error; change-point distance stratification) come from Gate 12 checkpoints dated
2026-08-28, which predate the mapping correction of 2026-09-28 (Q-5=(b), geometry dispatch in
`expert_plus_plus_attention.py`). Do the probes change when rerun against the current implementation,
and what are the current numbers?

A third probe (signal-dependence correlation over the twelve datasets) is computed from the suite
manifests; it is recomputed here from the same manifests for a complete, single-source §4.3 record.

## Reference

`plan/gates/gate12/gate12_results.json` + `gate12_summary.md` (historical; **read-only, never
overwritten**). Historical values that matter: delayed-copy copy-channel MSE Normal 0.00070–0.00079,
compressed 0.00127–0.00256, noise floor ≈1.045; change-point buckets (8,4) lowest in every bucket.

## Configuration (identical to Gate 12; one variable = the implementation version)

- Harness: copy of `plan/gates/gate12/gate12_mechanistic.py` in this directory. Training path imports
  the current `plan/gates/gate2/gate2_factorial.py` → `expert_plus_plus_attention.py` (post-fix).
- Variants: Normal, Flat=(4,4), DRRA=(8,4); ranks (64, 64) as in the gate3/gate2 protocol.
- Seed: 42 (single seed; the probes are descriptive by design — do not add seeds, that is out of scope).
- Task: L=256, H=96, 10 epochs fixed, batch 32, threads 8; synthetic families `syn_delayedcopy` and
  `syn_changepoints` generated by `gate3_datasets.py`.
- Cells kept: (i) per-channel test MSE on delayed-copy; (ii) per-window test MSE by rows-since-change
  buckets 0–100/100–500/500–1000/1000–2000; (iii) signal-dependence Pearson/Spearman vs lag-1
  autocorrelation and roughness over the twelve datasets from the frozen suite manifests.

## Predeclared decision rule (fixed now, not after seeing numbers)

1. The regenerated values replace the historical ones in §4.3 (owner-first), and Gate 12's stay as
   development history in its own directory. The "as-run historical / rerun is future work" wording is
   deleted from the paragraph once the probe is regenerated.
2. If a qualitative statement flips (e.g. (8,4) no longer lowest in every change-point bucket, or the
   copy-channel ordering changes), the text is updated to the new reading, not the old one.
3. If a value lands outside its historical bucket, that is reported as-is, with the same descriptive
   framing; no mechanism claim is strengthened beyond what the probe shows (acceptance per R5).
