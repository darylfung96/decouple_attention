# C2 Sweep Log — 2024–2026 routing/compression-attention literature

**Purpose:** structured record of every candidate examined for claim C2 ("to our knowledge, none of these works systematically varies the content-compression rate and the routing resolution independently and reports their separate main effects on a fixed task"). Falsification rubric: a paper COUNTERS C2 iff (1) it has BOTH a content/KV-compression knob AND a routing-resolution/selection knob, (2) it varies them INDEPENDENTLY (factorial/ablation grid), and (3) it reports SEPARATE main effects on a fixed task.
