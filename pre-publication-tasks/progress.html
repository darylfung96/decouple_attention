Overall story

Three rounds of work are finished. First, audits proved the forecast uses only the last history position and that the router receives zero learning signal. Second, a mapping bug was found that sent three of four test cells to the wrong history blocks; it was fixed, the correct cell was proven bit-identical before and after, and all 135 affected runs were redone. Third, the manuscript was updated throughout with corrected numbers and redrawn figures. The corrected headline: the three correctly routed cells tie within noise, the routing-matters finding survives, and there is no single winning cell. A 60-run replication on three new datasets is in flight.

B2 — Router learning signal

What changed: A one-step gradient audit measured every parameter and found all router settings get exactly zero gradient, because the hard top-k cutoff cannot carry a learning signal. The paper now states openly that routing is frozen at startup, in both the methods and the limitations.
