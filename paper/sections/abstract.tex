\begin{abstract}
Long-horizon time-series forecasting with Transformers is dominated by the
cost of quadratic attention over long lookback windows. Compressed-and-indexed
attention---compressing the key/value (KV) stream and routing each query to a
few compressed blocks---reduces this cost, but its design space is rarely
studied systematically. We treat the family as a two-axis design problem: the
KV compression rate $c_{KV}$ and the routing resolution $c_R$. Under a frozen
direct-96-step forecasting protocol with twenty-five paired seeds, we run a
parameter-equalized $2\times2$ factorial on ETTh1 and a three-way
comparison---full attention, the equal-rate CSA configuration $(4,4)$ (the
design point of DeepSeek-V4's Compressed Sparse Attention), and the dual-rate
corner DRRA $(8,4)$---across twelve datasets spanning the ETT family, large
multivariate series, different dynamics, and mechanistic synthetic probes. We
report four findings. First, on ETTh1 \emph{every} compressed configuration
outperforms full causal attention on validation MSE (mean deltas $-18.8\%$
to $-27.0\%$, in 95/100 paired seed comparisons; Holm-adjusted
$p \le 3.3\times10^{-6}$) at an equal or slightly smaller parameter
count; a bit-exact wiring audit confirms the baseline is correctly
implemented, and the advantage survives a dense retrain whose output
projection is matched to the compressed cells' rank; removing the router's
selection costs $12$--$27\%$ relative accuracy at identical parameters and
MAC count, but the selection-free cells still beat full attention.
Second, coarsening the router ($c_R{:}4{:}1 \to 8{:}1$)
systematically deteriorates accuracy ($+0.030$ validation MSE), while
coarsening KV compression is mildly beneficial ($-0.032$): the pre-registered
decoupling hypothesis is supported on ETTh1 (per-seed paired contrast, exact Wilcoxon $p = 0.0074$)---
and the conditional reading localizes it, since the interaction is carried by the
$(4,8)$ cell (fine content, coarse router: $+0.0662$) and at coarse content the
finer router adds nothing. Routing here is non-learned (the hard top-$k$ selector
gives the indexer no task gradient, though route membership still follows the drift
of upstream features), so this is a selection-architecture effect, not learned routing.
Third, the dual-rate corner DRRA $(8,4)$ reduces the attention matrix to 48 (+1 sink)
columns
versus 256 (5.22$\times$) at a matching parameter count. Fourth, the advantage
is \emph{signal-dependent}: 8/12 datasets each (DRRA and CSA), concentrated
on smooth, redundant real-world series, with slight losses on the synthetic
exact-recall probes. The factorial conclusions are statistically established
at $n{=}25$ on ETTh1 (a three-dataset replication did not reproduce the sign structure); the cross-dataset pattern remains descriptive (five seeds per
dataset, per-dataset tests capped at $p = 0.0625$), and latency is reported
for the development machine only.
\end{abstract}
