\section{Documented negative results}
\label{sec:appendix-negatives}
Six failed designs were tried and documented during the development of this
family (each with runnable or archived implementations and failure modes):
sparse-gather routing ($\sim$5$\times$ slower than dense masking on CPU);
a hierarchical (multi-scale pyramid) indexer (consistently worse than the
flat indexer); a multi-scale KV pyramid at 224 KV tokens
(overparameterized for the budget); a learnable block-bias offset (NaN
instability in $\sim$50\% of runs; root cause later traced to uninitialized
memory---`torch.empty' biases---and fixed); a linear-pool indexer with
gated residual KV (loses the per-head $\times$ per-dimension routing
flexibility of the softmax-overlap gate); and stacked $2{:}1 \rightarrow
2{:}1$ compression (harder to optimize within the budget). Negative results
of this kind are a deliberate part of the reproducibility package; they are
secondary to the paper's core claim.

\section{Surrogate attention (separate line of work)}
\label{sec:appendix-surrogate}
A separate pipeline distills the attention block's output into a cheap
student that replaces attention at inference: a per-token MLP student
($5.3\times$ faster, $+31\%$ validation MSE---per-token functions cannot
replicate cross-token routing) and a windowed-plus-global-prototypes
student ($2.18\times$ faster, ${\sim}0.4\%$ gap to its teacher at a
5+1-epoch distillation schedule, ${\sim}82\%$ fewer parameters than the
teacher). These numbers predate the frozen task and are not apples-to-apples
with the results in Section~\ref{sec:experiments}; the surrogate line is
kept in this paper only to acknowledge the overlap with attention
distillation work \citep{goldstein2025radlads} and is intended for a
separate publication \todo{decide retention: align distillation epochs to

\section{Reproducibility}
\label{sec:appendix-repro}
Every number in this paper traces to a repository artifact: the frozen task
specification (the sole authority on task definition), the pre-registered
factorial design, the run harness and its machine-readable results manifest
(20 factorial runs + 5 Normal-baseline runs, JSONL), a verified
per-module parameter registry, causality-verification and wiring-audit
suites (all variants pass future-invariance and exact-zero-future-gradient
checks; the Normal baseline is bit-identical to a naive causal reference),
and a sanity-test suite covering shapes, gradients, parameter counts, and
tiny-task fitting. Reported means and deltas are computed from the audited
manifests; the figure-generation scripts in this paper's source tree read
the same manifests, so every figure is regenerable.

\section{Roadmap}
\label{sec:appendix-roadmap}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Statistics (Gate 4):} 5--10 paired seeds; mixed-effects
        model with $\beta_2 > \beta_1$ as the main claim; TOST or Bayesian
        ROPE for any equivalence statement; paired Wilcoxon/Friedman with
        Holm correction; block bootstrap or HAC-robust tests for temporal
        dependence.
  \item \textbf{Datasets (Gate 3):} ETT family (ETTh2, ETTm1, ETTm2), large
        multivariate (Electricity, Traffic, Weather), different dynamics
        (Exchange, Solar-Energy, PEMS), plus a mechanistic synthetic; H and
        L sweeps.
  \item \textbf{Baselines:} PatchTST, iTransformer, DLinear, Informer,
        TimesNet, plus a linear and an SSM baseline under the identical
        frozen protocol.
  \item \textbf{Hardware/latency (Gate 5):} p50/p90/p99 with bootstrap CIs,
        throughput, FLOPs and memory accounting, scaling sweeps
        ($L \in \{128, \dots, 4096\}$), and cross-hardware transfer
        (Apple Silicon, x86 CPU, GPU).
  \item \textbf{Literature re-audit:} fresh 2024--2026 sweep of
        routing/compression-attention and TS-efficiency work before locking
        the title; resolve the outstanding citation-verification items
        flagged in the related-work section.
\end{enumerate}
